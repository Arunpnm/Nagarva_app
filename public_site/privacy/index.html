Nagarva is a business management platform for packers and movers companies in India, operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us", "Nagarva"). This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and what rights you have over it.

It is written to meet our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it.

Two different roles. Nagarva handles two kinds of data very differently. For a vendor's own account we are the Data Fiduciary and answerable to them directly. For the details of a vendor's customers — the people they are moving — the vendor is the Data Fiduciary and we act only as a Data Processor on their instructions. Section 3 explains what that means for you.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Vendors — packers and movers businesses who register for a Nagarva account.
  • Vendor staff — supervisors and employees a vendor adds to their account.
  • Customers of vendors — people who receive a survey, quotation, signature or tracking link from a vendor using Nagarva.
  • Website visitors — anyone browsing nagarva.in.

2. Data we collect from vendors

Account and business information

  • Business name, address, GST number and branch details
  • Owner's name, email address and mobile number
  • Password (stored only as a cryptographic hash, never in readable form)
  • Staff names, roles and PINs (PINs stored as bcrypt hashes)

Operational data you enter

  • Leads, surveys, quotations, orders, invoices and payment records
  • Expenses, salary ledgers, vehicle and trip records
  • Any notes, photographs or documents you upload

Technical data

  • Login timestamps and session information
  • Device and app version, for support and crash diagnosis
  • Error logs

3. Data about your customers

When a vendor uses Nagarva to record a customer's move, the platform stores whatever that vendor enters or that the customer submits — typically name, phone number, email, pickup and delivery addresses, moving dates, household item lists, photographs and electronic signatures.

We do not decide what is collected or why. The vendor does. We store and process it on their behalf, under their instructions, solely to provide the service. We do not use customer data for our own purposes, do not sell it, and do not use it to market to anyone.

If you are a customer of a packers and movers company and want your data corrected or erased, contact that company directly — they control it. If you cannot reach them, contact us at the address in Section 12 and we will help you identify and reach the right party.

4. Why we process data

  • To provide the service — running your account, storing your records, generating quotes, invoices and links.
  • To authenticate you — verifying your email, managing login sessions and PINs.
  • To communicate — verification emails, password resets, service notices and support replies.
  • To bill you — managing trials, subscriptions and payments.
  • To keep the platform secure — detecting abuse, investigating incidents, maintaining audit records.
  • To meet legal obligations — tax, accounting and any lawful request from an authority.

We rely on your consent, given when you register and accept this policy, and on the legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act. You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 8.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the platform, and only as far as necessary:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage
  • Netlify — website and public page hosting
  • Resend — sending transactional email such as verification and password reset messages
  • WhatsApp Business API providers — where a vendor chooses to send messages to their customers through the platform
  • Payment processors — where subscription payments are collected

We may also disclose data where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, and to protect our rights or the safety of users.

6. Where data is stored

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure which may be located outside India. Where personal data is transferred outside India, we do so in accordance with the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Central Government. Our providers are contractually required to maintain appropriate security.

7. How long we keep it

  • Active accounts — for as long as the account is open.
  • Closed accounts — deleted or anonymised within [NUMBER] days of closure, except where we must retain records to meet legal or tax obligations.
  • Financial records — retained as required by Indian tax law, currently eight years.
  • Survey and signature links — tokens expire 14 days after issue and become unusable.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, unless we are required to keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time. Note that withdrawing consent to essential processing means we can no longer provide the service.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 12) before approaching the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate — appoint another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

We will respond to a valid request within [NUMBER] days.

9. Security

We take reasonable safeguards to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) across the platform
  • Row-level security in the database, so each vendor's data is isolated from every other vendor's
  • Passwords and staff PINs stored only as cryptographic hashes
  • Single-use, time-limited tokens for customer-facing links
  • Access controls limiting staff to what their role requires

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by the DPDP Act.

10. Children

Nagarva is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email before they take effect. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
